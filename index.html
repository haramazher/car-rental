Good to know
Questions before the keys.
Exact requirements and terms depend on the rental provider and location.

        

          
A valid driver’s license, government-issued identification, and an accepted payment method are commonly requested. Confirm local requirements before pickup.

          
You can note a preference, but rental fleets are typically confirmed by vehicle category unless a provider guarantees a specific model.

          
The rental provider confirms the final daily rate, taxes, protection options, deposit, mileage policy, and any additional charges before booking.

          
Changes are subject to availability. Contact the provider as early as possible so they can confirm updated timing and any rate difference.
